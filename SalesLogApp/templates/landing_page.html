Good questions

Before you start tracking.

StewLog is built to make your commission easier to understand—not add another mystery.

How does StewLog estimate my commission?+

StewLog uses the sales you enter and the active pay-plan rules you review. Results are estimates for your own tracking and should be compared with your official payroll statement.

Can I use my dealership's pay plan?+

Yes. You can upload your plan, review the rules StewLog imports, make corrections, and activate it when you are satisfied.

Does it work well on my phone?+

Yes. StewLog is designed for mobile use, so you can log deals and check your month from the showroom or on the go.

Do I need a payment method for the trial?+

Yes. You will see the current plan and price before confirming, and a payment method is collected when you begin your 30-day trial.
